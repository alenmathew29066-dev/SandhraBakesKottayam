About Sandhra Bakes

Fresh, Delicious & Beautifully Designed Cakes

Sandhra Bakes is a home-based cake business in Kottayam, Kerala, creating fresh, delicious and beautifully designed cakes for birthdays, weddings, celebrations and special occasions.

We believe that celebrations are born from heart-to-heart moments. Rather than mass-produced bakery items, each cake at Sandhra Bakes is lovingly hand-crafted in our home kitchen, using only fresh, wholesome ingredients and tailored to your exact taste, theme, and celebration ideas.

Custom Designs

Attention to Detail
