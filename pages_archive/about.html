Our Kitchen Philosophy

Freshly Baked With Love, Never Mass-Produced

Located next to Manjamattam Church along the scenic Ayarkunnam Pallickathodu Road, Sandhra Bakes is an artisanal home-based bakery dedicated to elevating family milestones with honest, delicious homemade cakes.

Unlike commercial bakeries that rely on premade freezer sponges and artificial preservatives, every cake here is baked fresh exclusively upon order. We use real dairy butter, farm-fresh eggs, pure melted Belgian chocolates, and fresh fruits to ensure every slice tastes authentic and homely.

Pure & Fresh Ingredients

Real butter, wholesome milk cream, and genuine fruits with zero chemical shelf-life boosters.

Bespoke Custom Designs

Tailored themes for birthdays, weddings, baptisms, and romantic anniversaries.

Attention to Detail

Meticulous piping, velvety smooth textures, and balanced moisture in every layer.

Caring Personal Service

Direct communication with the baker to confirm your preferences and celebration schedule.
